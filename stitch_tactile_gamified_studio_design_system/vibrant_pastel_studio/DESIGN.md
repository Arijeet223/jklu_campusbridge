## Brand & Style

This design system embraces a buoyant, tactile, and gamified aesthetic rooted in soft playfulness and friendly approachability. Designed for creative tools, gamified productivity suites, and collaborative studios, the environment balances structured utility with cheerful physical delight. 

### Visual Movement & Tone
- **Tactile Gamification:** Surfaces mimic physical tokens, toy blocks, and candy-coated cards. Interactive elements convey pushable depth through crisp colored outlines and bottom-offset shadow shelves.
- **Friendly Modernism:** Replaces clinical digital austerity with generous curves, pillular silhouettes, and warm, inviting pastel accents.
- **Micro-Delight:** Every interaction reinforces momentum, tactile reward, and visual levity without sacrificing legibility or high-density information structuring.

## Colors

The palette derives from the soft confectionery swatches of the source palette, tuned with a 10% saturation boost to give interactive states clarity, bounce, and vibrant clarity against light canvas backdrops.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#F0B6D5`):** Candy pink blush. Anchors central action states, high-priority celebration badges, and primary selection rings.
- **Secondary (`#B5D9EC`):** Periwinkle-tinted sky pastel. Applied to navigation elements, active structural toggles, and contextual progress markers.
- **Tertiary (`#B6DEC9`):** Soft sage mint. Reserved for completion indicators, positive states, success metrics, and secondary chips.
- **Accent Lilac (`#CFBAED`):** Gamified badges, level achievements, and tertiary utility tags.
- **Accent Butter (`#F9E7B6`):** Warm spotlight banners, warning toasts, and coin counters.
- **Accent Peach (`#F6CDAF`):** Warm micro-counters, streak badges, and active tool indicators.
- **Canvas Neutral (`#FAF8F6`):** Warm creamy alabaster workspace ground, eliminating clinical white glare.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast, pure crisp container cards floating over the alabaster canvas.
- **Ink / Text Neutral (`#2D2633`):** Deep plum-charcoal ink, ensuring high-contrast AA/AAA accessibility over vibrant pastel buttons and crisp white cards.

## Typography

Typographic scale is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, wide aperture, and organic terminal curves to reinforce the studio's friendly, gamified character.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Extra Bold (`800`) and Bold (`700`) weights with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create dense, punchy titles that balance high-volume pastel shapes.
- **Body:** Kept at Medium (`500`) or Regular (`400`) weight in deep plum-charcoal (`#2D2633`) to ensure effortless scanning over creamy alabaster surfaces.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated weights (`700`) and subtle uppercase spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) when placed inside pill chips, tactile button faces, and gamified progress indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous physical breathing room, preventing the tactile components from feeling crowded.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column flexible grid system with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and `40px` (`2.5rem`) outer boundaries. Mobile displays snap to a 4-column system with `16px` gutters and `20px` margins.
- **Rhythm & Nesting:** Internal card padding scales between `space-md` (`16px`) for dense utility blocks and `space-xl` (`40px`) for studio workspace containers. Component gaps maintain an 8pt modular rhythm across child controls.
- **Safe Zones:** Gamified bottom action bars and floating studio toolbars float with `space-lg` separation above the canvas perimeter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on dark, realistic drop shadows. Instead, it utilizes physical "toy-like" extrusion, crisp pastel outlines, and directional bottom-shelf offsets.

### Depth Archetypes
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FAF8F6` alabaster background surface.
- **Level 1 (Crisp Card Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a continuous `1.5px` border in `#EADECE` or low-saturation pastel ink (`#E5D9E8`), paired with a crisp offset shelf: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #EAD7E2`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Modules):** Floating utility cards and panels using an increased shelf offset: `box-shadow: 0 6px 0 #E3CFDC`.
- **Level 3 (Tactile Pressable Buttons):** Solid interactive buttons featuring a `3px` downward physical extrusion: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 rgba(45, 38, 51, 0.15)`. On `:active`, elements translate down `3px` with shadow reduced to `0 1px 0`, creating a distinct mechanical press.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a diffused ambient pastel tint: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(181, 217, 236, 0.4), 0 4px 0 #D5C2CF`.

## Shapes

The shape system enforces pillular geometry (`roundedness: 3`).

### Structural Form
- **Base Components:** Action buttons, search fields, status chips, and control handles adopt maximum pill caps (`rounded-full` or `9999px`).
- **Cards & Studio Panels:** Card containers strictly use expansive `2rem` (`32px`, `rounded-3xl`) to `3rem` (`48px`) corner radiuses, eliminating harsh angles.
- **Nested Containers:** Inner list items and media wells scale inward gracefully using `1rem` to `1.5rem` radiuses to preserve optical concentricity with parent cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid candy pink (`#F0B6D5`), `1.5px` tactile stroke (`#DE95BE`), text in deep plum (`#2D2633`), font `label-lg`. Elevated with a `0 4px 0 #D48BB4` bottom extrusion.
- **Secondary:** Sky pastel (`#B5D9EC`), `1.5px` stroke (`#96C4DC`), bottom extrusion `0 4px 0 #85B5CE`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1.5px` border (`#EADECE`), bottom extrusion `0 3px 0 #E3D3C3`.
- **Active State:** `transform: translateY(3px)` with reduced `1px` shadow to convey true mechanical clickability.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `6px 14px`, text `label-md`.
- Styled using pastel category tints: Mint (`#B6DEC9`), Lilac (`#CFBAED`), or Peach (`#F6CDAF`) with matched 1px semi-opaque borders and dark plum typography.

### Input Fields
- Outer container filled with `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill (`rounded-full`), `1.5px` tactile border in `#E5D9E8`.
- Text styled in `body-md` deep plum (`#2D2633`) with `#9E92A6` placeholder text.
- Focus state expands a `3px` pastel glow ring in `#F0B6D5` with border shifting to `#DE95BE`.

### Cards & Studio Blocks
- Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `32px` (`rounded-3xl`), padded with `24px` to `32px`.
- Enclosed with a `1.5px` perimeter stroke (`#EFE4DF`) and supported by a soft 4px bottom shelf (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #EADECE`).
- Section breaks inside cards use soft dashed pastel dividers (`2px dashed #F2E8E4`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** `24px` squircle container (`rounded-lg`), `2px` border in `#CFBAED`. Checked state fills with `#CFBAED` showing a bold `#2D2633` checkmark with `0 2px 0 rgba(45, 38, 51, 0.12)` shadow.
- **Radio:** `24px` circle (`rounded-full`), `2px` border in `#B5D9EC`. Selected state reveals an interior `12px` solid candy pink circle (`#F0B6D5`).

### Gamified Studio Specials
- **Level/Streak Meters:** Pill track container filled with `#F2EBE6`, inner active bar filled with gradient from `#F0B6D5` to `#F6CDAF`, capped with a rounded edge and 2px top-highlight sheen.
- **Floating Tool Docks:** Pure white pill bar (`rounded-full`) floating with `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 38, 51, 0.08), 0 3px 0 #EADECE`.
